MASTEC (Association of MCA)

MASTEC is the MCA students' and staff association focused on overall personality development, technical skills, communication, innovation and industry exposure. It conducts technical events, quizzes, talks, seminars, competitions and co-curricular programmes throughout the academic year.

Association Activities
