Department of Computer Application

The BCA (Hons.) is a four-year undergraduate programme focused on programming, software development, databases, web development, AI, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and other emerging technologies. It combines theory, practical training, projects, internships, and industry exposure to develop technical, problem-solving, and career skills for the IT industry.

The MCA Regular Program, started in 2005 with an intake of 60 students, focuses on developing industry-ready computer professionals while providing strong infrastructure and an environment for pursuing interests in computing. It is a two-year, six-semester program combining intensive theory and practical training in multiple programming languages and applications. The department has experienced faculty from industry and academia, along with innovative teaching methods that develop analytical, problem-solving, communication, teamwork, confidence, time management, and career-building skills. It also provides modern infrastructure, including LCD-equipped classrooms and advanced computer labs with high-end machines.

Bachelor of Computer Applications (Honours)

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA Hons.) at MCET is a four-year undergraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. Designed to meet the evolving demands of the digital era, the programme equips students with a strong foundation in computer science, software development, and emerging technologies while nurturing innovation, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills. The curriculum integrates academic excellence with experiential learning, enabling students to master programming, software engineering, data management, cloud technologies, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and modern computing practices. Through a blend of classroom instruction, laboratory sessions, industry internships, research activities, and live projects, students graduate as industry-ready professionals capable of addressing real-world technological challenges. Master of Computer Application

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a two-year postgraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. The programme is designed to develop highly skilled computing professionals capable of addressing the evolving challenges of the global technology ecosystem. With a strong emphasis on academic excellence, innovation, and industry relevance, the programme provides an advanced understanding of computer science, software engineering, intelligent computing, cloud technologies, data analytics, and secure computing. Students are equipped with the knowledge and practical competencies required to design, develop, deploy, and manage modern software systems and digital solutions. The curriculum follows a balanced approach that integrates theoretical foundations with hands-on learning through advanced laboratories, software development practices, technical seminars, domain expertise workshops, research activities, and major projects. This holistic learning environment enables students to cultivate technical proficiency, critical thinking, problem-solving ability, professional ethics, and leadership skills, preparing them for successful careers in the IT industry, research organizations, and technology-driven enterprises.
