About Our Institution

A strong foundation for academic excellence, innovation and professional development.

Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology

Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology is situated in Nedumangad, Kerala, India and was established in 2002.

The college is approved by AICTE and affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. The institution is accredited with NAAC-A Grade and provides a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

With a strong focus on teaching, research, innovation, extracurricular activities and industry exposure, the institution prepares students to meet the challenges of a rapidly evolving professional world.
