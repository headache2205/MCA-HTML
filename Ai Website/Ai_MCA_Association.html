About MASTEC

Association of MCA Students & Staff

MASTEC is an association consisting of MCA students and staff. It focuses on the overall personality development of students and provides a platform for developing technical abilities as well as broader personal skills.

The association regularly conducts invited lectures, seminars, webinars, cultural programmes and technical events for MCA students.

MASTEC Inauguration 2026

21 January 2026 10:30 AM – 12:15 PM

The Department of Master of Computer Applications at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology successfully conducted the MASTEC Inauguration 2026 on Wednesday, 21 January 2026. The programme formally inaugurated the association's activities for the academic year and was intended to encourage students through academic, technical and co-curricular initiatives.

Mr. Arif Mohammed, Director of JHF IT Innovations, attended the programme as Chief Guest. His presence brought an industry-oriented perspective to the inauguration.

The programme began at 10:30 AM with a prayer and was followed by a welcome address from Prof. Sreeja K., HoD in charge of the MCA Department.

The inaugural programme included the following:

  • Presidential Address by V. Suresh Babu, Principal.
  • Address by Director Sreekanth Narayanan.
  • Presentation of the MASTEC report by Subha Ramachandran, Faculty Advisor.
  • Release of the MASTEC Anthem and Website.
  • Prize Distribution.
  • Inaugural Address by Arif Mohammed, Director, JHF IT Innovations.
  • Vote of Thanks by Dani M. Panicker, Secretary, MASTEC.

The programme concluded successfully at 12:15 PM.

Start Up Idea Competition '25

Pixel2Prototype Innovation & Entrepreneurship

The MCA Department, under the MASTEC banner, organized Pixel2Prototype, a startup idea competition intended to encourage innovation, entrepreneurship and problem-solving among students.

The programme encouraged students to transform creative concepts into practical prototype-oriented ideas.

Objectives
  • Encourage innovation and creativity among students.
  • Promote an entrepreneurial mindset and startup culture.
  • Give students a platform for presenting solutions to real-world problems.
  • Connect conceptual ideas with practical implementation.

Students registered online and submitted their innovative startup concepts within the specified deadline. The ideas addressed real-world challenges and were assessed on originality, feasibility, innovation and potential impact.

The competition encouraged students to go beyond textbook learning and use their technical knowledge creatively.

🏆 Top Idea Award — Cash Prize ₹1000/-

The Top Idea Award was won by Mohammed Aminshad from the AI & ML stream. His concept was recognized for its innovation, feasibility and potential real-world impact.

Pixel2Prototype became an important MASTEC initiative for encouraging students to turn innovative thoughts into structured startup concepts. It also supported the development of an entrepreneurial environment within the institution and encouraged students to pursue innovation-oriented careers.

The Department of MCA plans to continue similar initiatives to strengthen industry-relevant skills and entrepreneurial exposure among students.

Technical Talk

28 April 2025 9:30 AM – 11:00 AM Mini Auditorium

  • J R Aaliya: Cyber Security and Networking, including important challenges and methods for protecting digital infrastructure.
  • Nandana Keshav: Synthetic Data Generation and its importance in data science and artificial intelligence applications.

Pre-Placement Talk – Guiding MCA Students Towards Career Readiness

17 March 2025 MCA Seminar Hall

MASTEC organized a Pre-Placement Talk on 17 March 2025 at the MCA Seminar Hall.

The session was conducted by Mr. Manu Manohar, Chief Operations Officer, Kompetenzen. He discussed industry expectations, employability skills and strategies for performing effectively during placement drives.

The session emphasized preparation for interviews, aptitude tests and group discussions, while also highlighting communication skills, confidence and continuous learning as important factors for career development.

Students were able to clarify their questions, gain practical information about corporate culture and understand the importance of connecting academic learning with real-world requirements.

The event was beneficial to MCA students and encouraged them to take proactive steps towards placement preparation. The Department of MCA expressed its appreciation to Mr. Manu Manohar and the participating students.

Technical Talk on MERN Full Stack Development

13 August 2024 1:30 PM – 2:30 PM

MASTEC organized a Technical Talk on MERN Full Stack Development on 13 August 2024. The session was delivered by Ms. Fathima Nizar, Software Specialist at Networkz Systems.

The programme was designed to give students practical knowledge of MERN, consisting of MongoDB, Express.js, React.js and Node.js, which is an important skill set in modern software development.

Ms. Fathima Nizar explained the principal components of MERN technology, discussed real-world applications and described career opportunities associated with the technology stack.

The session received a positive response from MCA students, who actively interacted with the speaker.

The Department of MCA expressed its gratitude to Networkz Systems and Ms. Fathima Nizar for their support and contribution.
