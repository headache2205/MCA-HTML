About the HOD

Prof. Sreeja K

Prof. Sreeja K obtained her Master of Computer Applications (MCA) from Government Engineering College, Thrissur, Kerala.

She joined Mohandas College of Engineering in 2003 as a System Analyst. During her tenure, she was responsible for monitoring and analysing the campus automation software, designing and administering the campus network, and managing data integration across the institution.

Since 2007, she has been serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of MCA. From 2014 onwards, she has also been serving as the Head of the Department of MCA.

Prior to joining MCET, she worked as a System Analyst at the State Project Director's Office, DPEP, Trivandrum, where she headed the State MIS Unit of the project implementation body.

Her responsibilities included educational planning, budget preparation, monitoring and analysis of key project variables, and coordinating project data and achievements across various fields.
