About the Department

Building Tomorrow's Technology Professionals

The MCA regular program started functioning in 2005 with a sanctioned strength of sixty students. The program is committed to producing industry-ready professionals while providing an inspiring academic environment for students interested in computers and related fields.

The Master of Computer Applications is an intensive two-year postgraduate program combining theoretical knowledge with extensive practical learning across six semesters.

Our focus: Developing analytical thinking, technical expertise, communication abilities, teamwork and professional confidence.

Why Choose MCA?

  • Industry-oriented technical education
  • Hands-on practical learning
  • Exposure to modern programming languages
  • Experienced faculty from academia and industry
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving development
  • Professional and personality development
